# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Voice AI is a personal macOS utility for dictation and voice control while working in other apps. The user expects it to stay out of the way, respond to two global shortcuts, and make recovery obvious when a permission or local model is unavailable.

## Product Purpose

Hold the dictation shortcut for locally transcribed text on the clipboard, or toggle the second shortcut for voice control. Success means the menu-bar state is truthful, Settings are reachable, configuration survives relaunches, and both modes work without Docker.

## Brand Personality

Calm, native, discreet. Superwhisper is the interaction-quality reference, without copying its branding.

## Anti-references

Malformed custom menus, backend dashboards, diagnostic clutter in the primary surface, silent failures, decorative motion, and unfamiliar controls that fight macOS conventions.

## Design Principles

- Make the current state truthful.
- Keep primary actions one click away.
- Use native macOS behavior before custom UI.
- Preserve the user's transcript and configuration on failure.
- Put diagnostics behind Settings, not in the everyday workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow macOS appearance and semantic colors, support keyboard navigation and VoiceOver labels, respect Reduce Motion, and never rely on color alone for status. Permission failures must include a plain-language recovery action.
